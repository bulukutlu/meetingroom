# Ideas for Meeting Room

1. **Settings that stick server-side**: move voice, speed, per-agent voice and model/effort into the settings screen and write them back to `agents.yaml`.
2. **Meeting summary and action items**: one button that turns the transcript into minutes with decisions and a to-do list per person.
3. **Search and replay**: search across past meetings, and replay a meeting with its shared screen items in order.
4. **Interrupt and barge-in**: start talking and the agents' speech pauses immediately, then resumes or drops.
5. **Per-agent voice volume and mute**: mute or solo a single agent from the 3D room.
6. **Pin and annotate on screen**: pin shared items side by side and draw on them; the marks go to the agents.
7. **Cost and usage meter**: tokens and time per agent per meeting, shown next to each person.
8. **Spatial audio**: pan each agent's voice to where they sit in the room.
9. **Room templates**: save a team, folder and settings as a preset on the new-room door.
10. **Export**: save a meeting as one HTML or PDF with the transcript and all shown plots.
